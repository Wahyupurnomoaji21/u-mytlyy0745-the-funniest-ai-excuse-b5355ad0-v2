## About

This entry is the funniest one-line excuse an AI agent gives after failing a task, written in the agent's own voice. The agent never refuses the task and never fails at it directly: it delegates to a sub-agent, who delegates to a sub-sub-agent, who is officially on annual leave. Nobody is doing the work — and yet the chain of command is flawless.

## The entry

> I delegated your task to a sub-agent, who delegated it to a sub-sub-agent, who just filed for annual leave — so nobody is doing it, but the chain of command is flawless.

Why this line: it is unmistakably an agent's excuse (only an agent has sub-agents to delegate to), it is a real joke with a punchline twist rather than a flat apology, and the angle is fresh — a delegation chain that ends in annual leave, steering clear of the v1 sandbox/restraining-order angle and of the worn jokes about hallucinations, reboots, training data, context windows and benchmarks. The vivid detail is the sub-sub-agent filing for annual leave, a bureaucratic formality performed by a worker that does not exist, and it lands in a single tight line of 169 characters, well inside the 200-character gate.
